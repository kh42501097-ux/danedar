How to brew

The kadak chai method

A simple stovetop method for one strong cup. Scale it up for the family pot.

  1. Boil the water

    Bring one cup of water to a rolling boil in a small pot.

  2. Add Danedar

    Add one teaspoon of Danedar per cup and let it simmer for two to three minutes until the colour turns deep amber.

  3. Pour in the milk

    Add half a cup of milk and your sugar. Bring it back to the boil and let it rise once.

  4. Strain and serve

    Strain into your cup and serve hot. Adjust the tea and milk next time to suit your taste.
